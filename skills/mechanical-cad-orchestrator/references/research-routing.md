# Research routing

Research only when it can change geometry, material, process, or validation:
an unfamiliar mechanism; a standard or vendor component; strength, thermal,
chemical, or safety concern; material/process constraints; or an uncertain
failure mode. Query in the form `object + mechanism + process + failure mode`.
Start with the exact part and broaden only to analogous parts.

Prefer sources in this order: official CAD/manufacturer/standards documents;
manufacturer application notes; technical publications; then community reports
for practical setup evidence. Treat community advice as conditional on printer,
material, machine, and calibration. Respect licenses and copyrights: do not
copy whole books or build a bulk corpus from them.

Store a compact source card only after it repeatedly improves shipped designs:
the trigger, applicable conditions, recommendation, counterexamples, and link.
Otherwise keep a one-time web finding in the job report. This preserves tokens
and prevents stale, generic RAG from overruling current evidence.
